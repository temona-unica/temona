Prof. Luciano Colombo

Statistical Mechanics Master's degree

Meccanica Statistica • Physics, University of Cagliari • Year 1, first semester • 9 ECTS

Statistical mechanics as a unified framework linking the microscopic description of matter to thermodynamics. From the foundations (ensembles, ergodicity, the H-theorem, entropy) the course moves to classical and quantum systems at equilibrium, from Fermi–Dirac and Bose–Einstein gases to phonons and the Ising model, and closes with non-equilibrium phenomena: Boltzmann equation, linear response, Green–Kubo and Onsager relations, Brownian motion.

The Structure of Reality

La struttura del reale • Pontificia Facoltà Teologica della Sardegna • 48 hours

A course on four topics: the epistemology of the scientific method, modern cosmology, artificial intelligence and the natural sciences, and science and technology. Lectures are combined with guided-discussion laboratories.

Prof. Claudio Melis

Structure of Matter Bachelor's degree

Struttura della Materia • Physics, University of Cagliari • Year 3, second semester • 9 ECTS

An introduction to the physics of atoms, molecules and crystals, with elements of classical and quantum statistical physics. Three modules cover atomic physics (from hydrogenic to many-electron atoms, the laser), molecular physics (bonding, rotational and vibrational spectra, Raman scattering) and solid state physics (phonons, band theory, metals and semiconductors), with a phenomenological approach and extensive problem solving.

Prof. Riccardo Dettori

Quantum Theory of Matter Master's degree

Teoria Quantistica della Materia • Physics, University of Cagliari • Second semester • 6 ECTS

Density functional theory (DFT), one of the most widely used methods for modelling atoms, molecules and solids at the quantum level. The course covers its foundations (Hohenberg–Kohn theorems, Kohn–Sham equations, exchange-correlation functionals, time-dependent DFT) and its practical use to compute structural, vibrational, electronic, optical and magnetic properties, with an introduction to ab initio molecular dynamics.

Dr. Antonio Cappai

Nanophysics Master's degree

Nanofisica • Physics, University of Cagliari • Second semester • 6 ECTS

The physics of electrons and transport at the nanoscale, where confinement and coherence lengths become comparable to the system size. The course covers quantum confinement in low-dimensional systems, the Boltzmann equation and carrier scattering, Green's functions and diagrammatic methods, and ballistic and coherent transport (Landauer–Büttiker formalism, resonant tunnelling, Aharonov–Bohm effect, localization).
