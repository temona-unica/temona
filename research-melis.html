Prof. Claudio Melis • Department of Physics • University of Cagliari

Claudio Melis

Associate Professor

Web • Scholar • ORCID

2D MOFs for thermoelectrics

Multiscale platform linking coordination chemistry, two-dimensional morphology and thermoelectric performance.

Supported by two PNRR cascade projects (SMOS-MOF and MOF2D-TED).

Hydrodynamic and non-diffusive heat transport

Regimes where the standard diffusive picture breaks down: collective phonon flow, wave-like heat propagation and second sound in semiconductors, two-dimensional materials and confined systems, together with the non-equilibrium protocols developed to describe them.

Phonon engineering in 2D materials and superlattices

Pristine and hydrogenated graphene, TMDCs with controlled defects and stacking, moiré superlattices.
